Standalone visual study

Illustrations for perception
and natural behavior

Four rendered directions for replacing the current orbit. These are visual prototypes only; none are connected to the site navigation.

01 / ILLUSORY CONTOUR

The brain completes the world

Incomplete forms create a clear object through negative space, making perception itself the illustration.

02 / EXPLORATION BEHAVIOR

Behavior is a path through possibility

A continuous trajectory branches around cues in the environment, showing action as active sampling.

03 / NATURAL WORLD

An animal is never out of context

Layered contours, environmental signals, and an animal silhouette place behavior inside a living world.

04 / PERCEPTION–ACTION

Sensation becomes behavior

A continuous loop connects sensory input, neural interpretation, action, and learning.
